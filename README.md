## Purpose

ARFI examines American institutions beneath current political headlines, asking what must be protected, what may lawfully change, what measurable outcomes should improve, and how reforms can remain correctable.

This manuscript is **not** a political-party platform, an authorized government program, or a ratified policy package. It distinguishes existing rights from proposed guarantees, legal authority from preferred outcomes, evidence from hypothesis, and scenarios from forecasts.
