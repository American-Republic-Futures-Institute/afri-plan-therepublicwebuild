## Provenance

The executive summary and introduction synthesize six foundational interviews and the unratified *Foundational Charter and First Principles*, ARFI-US2100-FOUND-001 v0.1.0. Earlier draft L0, L1, L2, security, economic and governance models remain unratified. The charter and machine-readable models have not yet been imported into this plan repository; references to them identify their provenance, not a claim that they are hosted here.

## Claim classifications and editorial controls

Classify material claims as FACT, LAW, NORMATIVE, HYPOTHESIS, SCENARIO, CONTESTED, or RECOMMENDATION. Preserve source dates, affected population, jurisdiction, method and material uncertainty. Public criticism is welcome; popularity or funding does not confer publication authority. AI may assist research, but accountable humans approve claims and revisions. Do not presume that a draft recommendation is enacted law, or that source material proves all causal assumptions attributed to it.

The introduction provides stable INT section markers and the foundational charter uses FP-01 through FP-20. A future comment service should reference stable paragraph identifiers at exact revisions.
