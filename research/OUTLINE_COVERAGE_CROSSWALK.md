## Cross-cutting topics and duplication rule

Many concerns span multiple domains (for example, education affects workforce, defense readiness and families). Every original chapter has one editorial home, but **may be referenced by any other domain or institution**. The authoritative factual, legal, and capability representations belong in the separate architecture repository. This editorial crosswalk is not a formal mapping of agency authorities and does not validate that an institution has jurisdiction over any proposed action.

## Review priorities

1. Confirm that each domain's future condition is aspirational rather than a hidden predetermined reform.
2. Confirm that institutional names are a current-state **navigation starting point**, not a prescription to perpetuate or abolish an organization.
3. Confirm that rights, uncertainty, and federalism constraints are preserved when one topic is referenced from several sections.
4. Reclassify any item later found to fit more naturally elsewhere by editing this crosswalk and preserving its immutable chapter ID.
