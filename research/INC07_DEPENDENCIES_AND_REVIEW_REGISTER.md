## 1. Scope, authority and classification

This register documents the first **integrated, nonbinding** cross-domain research view for four existing, unratified national domains: education (`DOM-02`), health (`DOM-03`), economy (`DOM-05`), and cybersecurity/privacy (`DOM-12`). It reuses their stable proposed outcome identifiers and eight cross-domain capability IDs from INC-06. Remaining domains have **not** yet been assigned comprehensive dependencies.

The machine-readable architecture records:
- **39** candidate outcomes, **8** existing shared capabilities;
- **92** typed candidate links across five relationship classes;
- **10** hypothetical options, **10** legal/normative guardrails, **9** unresolved authority research gates and **9** qualitative scenarios;
- **9** published research/provenance records distinguishing primary text, official analysis, methodology and ARFI hypotheses.

These counts describe the **model**, not the American government's performance or policy consensus. All records are published for question development and public correction. There is no adopted implementation proposal, assigned agency owner, granted power, verified effect size, numeric probability or official national model certification.

**Immutable source contract:** The graph pins the merged manuscript at commit `d856e926f4180225fc74cb57ba770b6189016be3` and the INC-06 architecture baseline at `ac04222e2f543e12c1c460d6d5581a33ba9b4d2a`. Any renaming or removal of a domain outcome ID requires a versioned change and referential review, not silent substitution.

## 2. How to read an INC-07 connection

| Relation | Example | Status of claim | Required review before interpreting as fact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `supports` | Shared recovery capabilities may help `HLT-O08` service continuity. | Candidate enabling hypothesis | Evaluate actual clinical service continuity and alternative pathways. |
| `depends_on` | `EPR-O08` industrial resilience may depend on reliable production and supply networks. | Candidate prerequisite for inquiry | Test substitution, diversity, full costs and geographic exposure. |
| `conflicts_with` | `OPT-07-02` centralized mandatory identity may harm `CYB-O03` personal-data protection. | **Conditional downside of a specific option** | Compare forms of identity assurance and evidence of exclusion/privacy effects. |
| `constrained_by` | `OPT-07-03` education grant reporting is limited by State autonomy and lawful federal conditions. | Separate primary-law and ARFI normative lenses | Identify actual grant, law, jurisdiction and rights implications. |
| `requires_legal_authority` | `OPT-07-05` facility acquisition calls for property and statutory review. | **Unsatisfied review gate** | Specific enabling law, public-use and compensation questions, acquisition and fiscal legality. |

`conflicts_with` does **not** state that two desirable outcomes cannot coexist. A possible intervention, its mechanisms and its alternatives must be identified. `requires_legal_authority` means legal authority **has not been established** by this model, not that a named agency can lawfully do the act.

Evidence references `EVID-07-*` document the origin and *limits* of claims; none proves that an untested intervention will cause its intended benefit.

## 5. Rights, constitutional authorities and jurisdiction

The model keeps existing legal text separate from proposed human values and research preferences. Particular contexts require legal review:

**Legislation, expenditure and finance:** Congress's power to levy certain taxes and make appropriations is governed by constitutional text and law. An imagined finance mechanism cannot be treated as authorized because it would produce a desirable outcome.

**Funding conditions:** The Congressional Research Service identifies constitutional limits on Congress's ability to attach grant conditions—including notice, relationship, anti-coercion and independent constitutional limitations. Their application is context-specific. A State-run education system does not become a federal administrative office when it participates in a federally funded program.

**Private property:** Proposed compulsory purchase or acquisition of critical facilities raises public use, compensation, enacted statutory authority and ongoing public cost. Desirability of healthcare access does not nullify those inquiries.

**Privacy and expressive rights:** The First, Fourth, Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments have legally specific reach. A rights-aware program still requires lawful data authority and careful review of remedies and safeguards. The manuscript's broader aspiration for cognitive autonomy is a proposed principle, not a newly enacted right.

**Civilian and military roles:** Cyber defense, intelligence collection, civilian assistance and criminal investigation operate under different bodies of law. A crisis or technical ability alone does not authorize a new domestic mission.

**Tribal, State and territorial powers:** These derive from distinct legal structures. The graph neither fabricates unmodeled government agencies nor assigns them as subsidiaries of a federal department. Actual partnership or changes require their lawful participation as appropriate.

**Primary references:** [National Archives constitutional text](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript), [Bill of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript), [later amendments](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27), [CRS funding conditions study](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R46827/R46827.2.pdf), [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20). The latter is technical guidance, **not** legal authorization or proof of causal outcomes.

## 6. Human and machine change control

The architecture graph stores canonical IDs, pinned versions and their relationship claims. The manuscript is the public explanation. The website may display both and collect comments, but public comments do not directly amend either repository.

For a proposed change, record:

1. Which manuscript domain paragraph, outcome, capability, relationship, option or safeguard is implicated.
2. Whether the comment is **normative preference, empirical claim, legal interpretation, implementation proposal or correction**; multiple types may apply.
3. Source URL, date, precise claim, limitations, counter-evidence and affected communities, where available.
4. Whether a reply, an evidence investigation, a separate research option, a rejected change with reasons or a versioned model PR is appropriate.
5. Who reviewed and approved the change, whether independent legal or specialist review was obtained, and what remains unresolved.
6. Which downstream graph links, scenarios and public texts would require updating.

Statistical evidence and arguments from personal experience should both be considered appropriately; neither should be falsely represented as the other. No automated classifier should determine public ideological correctness or silently merge a policy.

## 7. INC-07 completion and unresolved decisions

| Decision | Why unresolved | Follow-on work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whether the eight capability abstractions are sufficiently distinct | Some share supporting infrastructure or data dependencies. | Architecture review, additional evidence and future domains. |
| Which claimed prerequisites are necessary rather than one optional path | Proposed dependency is not causal proof. | Comparative studies and case-specific domain review. |
| Whether a proposed legal route actually exists | This increment creates research gates, not opinions on specific implementability. | Primary statutes, controlling judgments, domain/jurisdiction-specific counsel. |
| How to define fair distribution across communities and people | National averages can disguise harm or unequal burden. | Representative data and public participation. |
| How to extend the graph to all 14 domains | Current edges are a bounded four-domain sample. | Additional reviewed future-state chapters. |
| How to share national models with public participants safely | Publication, moderation, authentication and editorial roles are separate. | INC-08 website/public-review design and institutional governance. |
| Whether graph relationships can support calibrated simulations | Hypotheses lack measured effect distributions, counterfactuals and calibration. | Future research program, not this increment. |

**Structural completion** requires that all typed endpoint references resolve, the legal gates remain explicitly unsatisfied, every hypothetical option has a relevant harm, safeguard and legal review link, no opinion is presented as an enacted right, and every public scenario includes what remains uncertain.

**Substantive completion** remains pending until qualified researchers, legal specialists, public stakeholders and affected governments can evaluate the actual claims.

The next practical stage is **INC-08 — Initial Public Review Release**: a navigable first collection with accessible source trails, paragraph-level review mechanisms, responses and visible uncertainty. Any new simulations, numerical targets, institutional restructurings or adopted recommendations should remain separate, specifically authorized research increments.

*This register records what ARFI is prepared to question, not what America has agreed to enact.*
