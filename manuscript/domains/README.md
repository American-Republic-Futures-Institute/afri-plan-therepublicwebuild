# Future-State Domain Pilot Chapters

**The Republic We Build | ARFI**  
**Status:** Initial INC-05 research pilots; draft, not ratified

The main manuscript begins with [foundational principles](../01-introduction-and-first-principles.md) and then examines the country Americans might seek to build across **national outcome domains**. These pilot chapters test whether an accessible, future-state-first method works across different subjects without prescribing government implementation.

| Domain | Pilot chapter | Distinct research question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DOM-03 — Health and Human Well-Being** | [Read the health future-state chapter](DOM-03-health-and-human-well-being.md) | How can people receive meaningful, effective care while retaining dignity, choice and affordable access, without predetermining service ownership? |
| **DOM-05 — Economy, Work and Prosperity** | [Read the prosperity future-state chapter](DOM-05-economy-work-and-prosperity.md) | How do productive markets, opportunity, citizen value and fiscal resilience work together without adopting a particular tax or corporate policy? |
| **DOM-12 — Cybersecurity, Privacy and Information Integrity** | [Read the cyber/privacy future-state chapter](DOM-12-cybersecurity-privacy-and-information-integrity.md) | How can a free society defend digital systems and meaningful personal privacy without turning security into control over lawful expression? |

The previously merged **[Education and Lifelong Learning (DOM-02)](../02-education-and-lifelong-learning.md)** chapter supplied the initial exemplar for this method.

## Editorial rules

The future-state chapter is the owner of its **national outcome and citizen impact**. Separate institutional profiles identify existing lawful roles and potential contributions. The research register owns source provenance; the technical architecture repository will ultimately own canonical cross-domain capability relationships.

No headline indicator has been assigned an unsupported numerical target. No proposed social guarantee, market instrument or surveillance authority is treated as enacted law. Public disagreement and evidence that changes a conclusion belong in the review record.

These drafts are ready for source, legal, practitioner and public review—not for adoption as specific policy or government implementation instructions.
