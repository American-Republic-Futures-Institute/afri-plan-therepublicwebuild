## Canonical authority and architecture boundaries

The [ARFI National Architecture](https://github.com/American-Republic-Futures-Institute/afri-architecture) repository is the **machine-readable institutional source**, including selected federal institutions, constitutional/legal authority models, independent State models and the [INC-06 institutional reference overlay](https://github.com/American-Republic-Futures-Institute/afri-architecture/tree/main/model/reference).

Do not treat a source catalog `parent_id`, shared-capability link, proposed future role, or conceptual domain outcome as permission for governmental action. A binding change requires the competent legal authority and applicable public processes; this manuscript does not supply them.

Specific State, tribal, territorial and local institutions require distinct source verification and participation. All proposal texts and outcome indicators remain open to public and expert review rather than ratified recommendations.
