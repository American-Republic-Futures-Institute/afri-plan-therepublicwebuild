# The Republic We Build — Incremental Manuscript Development Roadmap

**Document:** ARFI-PLAN-ROADMAP-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0 — proposed  
**Status:** Editorial sequence, not a committed government implementation calendar

## Objective

Develop the manuscript as an accessible, future-state-focused body of research, with policy domains and institutional responsibilities represented as **two navigational views of the same national design questions**. Advance in independently reviewable increments. Do not treat the absence of a verified nationwide baseline as permission to invent one.

## Working PR strategy

- Prefer one coherent manuscript increment per PR. Draft PRs permit discussion and substantive revision.
- The executive summary and introduction already reviewed are not altered merely to reorder later chapters.
- A later domain chapter may reference an institutional profile even before that profile is fully researched, but must mark the link as prospective.
- Research notes may be merged without publication of a policy recommendation; publication status and recommendation status are separate.
- Maintain source references, unresolved objections and document/version provenance in all review packages.
- No merge signifies constitutional approval, enactment or validation of economic or security outcomes.

## Human editorial gates

**Gate A — Architecture consistency:** Chapter maps to existing first principles and relevant future-state domains, has stable IDs and preserves distinctions between rights and national objectives.

**Gate B — Conceptual quality:** Future conditions, enabling capabilities and outcome-based progress checkpoints are clear without dictated agencies, technologies, laws or schedules.

**Gate C — Evidence quality:** Empirical and legal claims are sourced; historical evidence and inference are distinguished; unresolved questions remain explicit.

**Gate D — Democratic and institutional fairness:** Language respects public workers and institutions, recognizes meaningful public participation, and does not treat lawful disagreement as a defect.

**Gate E — Cross-view traceability:** Where grounded, chapter links point to institutional roles and vice versa; no unsupported attribution of legal authority.

**Gate F — Reader usability:** Clear hierarchy, accessible prose, navigable Markdown and reviewable structure. Detailed modeling lives in research records or the separate architecture repository.

## Immediate next authoring target

**INC-03 — Education and Lifelong Learning** is the first planned substantive domain exemplar because it is recognizable to the public and crosses families, state/local responsibilities, higher education, workforce, research and technology. The chapter should begin with applied learning and meaningful opportunity, not a proposed federal curriculum, agency reorganization, or predetermined funding mechanism.

Once the exemplar receives editorial feedback, update the templates before drafting the full set of domain chapters. Repeat the process for institutional profiles so the final paper remains coherent as coverage expands.
